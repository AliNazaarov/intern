# Calibrated Disagreement: Measuring Evaluator Drift in LLM Judges

#Calibrated Disagreement: Measuring Evaluator Draft in ELM Judges
**Authors:** Lena Marwick (Quillmetric AI), Tomas Achebe (Quillmetric AI), Rhea Duarte (Independent)
*Proceedings of the Workshop on Reliable Machine Learning Evaluation (RMLE '23), 2023*
**Abstract.** Automated judges built on large language models are increasingly used to score other models. We show that judge behavior drifts across prompt templates, model versions, and time, and that a single judge can reverse leaderboard rankings in 18% of pairwise comparisons. We propose calibrated disagreement, a panel-based method that estimates judge reliability without human labels, and release a benchmark of 9,600 annotated comparisons.
