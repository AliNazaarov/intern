# Tallyrun wants to tell small businesses what next month looks like *The Ledgerline, January 22, 2025 - by Priyesh Nandakumar*

Most small-business owners can tell you what is in their bank account today. Few can tell you what will be there in six weeks. Tallyrun, a New York startup foun ded by Mateo Ferreira-Lind, is trying to change that.

Ferreira-Lind, a former credit analyst, personally built the first version of Tallyrun's forecasting model in 2021, training it on anonymized invoice histories. The company now serves roughly 9,000 small businesses and processes about $2.1 billion in monthly invoice volume, according to figures Tallyrun shared with The Ledgerline.

"Late payments kill more small companies than bad products," Ferreira-Lind said. Tallyrun predicts when each customer invoice will actually be paid and alerts o wners before a cash gap appears.
