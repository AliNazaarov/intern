# Verdant Fold Therapeutics, Inc. - Employment Agreement (excerpt)

This Employment Agreement is entered into as of August 15, 2022 between Verdant Fold Therapeutics, Inc. (the "Company") and Hana Kowalczyk-Ito, Ph.D. ("Executive").

**1. Position.** Executive shall serve as the Company's **Chief Scientific Officer**, commencing **September 1, 2022**, and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer, Bram Toivonen.

**2. Duties.** Executive shall direct the Company's research programs, including target selection, computational chemistry, and preclinical biology, and shall serve on the Company's Scientific Advisory Board.

**3. Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an initial base salary of $230,000 per year, reviewed annually by the Board.

**4. Equity.** Executive shall be eligible for equity awards as determined by the Board.

Signed by both parties on August 15, 2022.
