# Mapping the pockets nobody else could find
*Helix Ledger, May 2025 issue - Profiles section, by Tamsin Okoro*

For two decades, certain proteins were considered "undruggable" because they lac ked the deep pockets that small molecules need to bind. Hana Kowalczyk-Ito think s many of them were simply mapped badly.
As Chief Scientific Officer of Boston-based Verdant Fold Therapeutics, Kowalczyk -Ito leads a team that uses simulations of protein motion to find temporary binding pockets. Since 2023, her team has identified three preclinical drug candidates, two in oncology and one in fibrosis, the company told Helix Ledger.

"The pocket is there for a fraction of a microsecond," she said. "You have to ca tch it moving."

Kowalczyk-Ito trained as a biophysicist and published the core method in 2021 be fore co-founding the company.
