# Society for Robotic Manipulation

April 2, 2024

Dear Dr. Strand,

Thank you for joining the Society for Robotic Manipulation. We are pleased to confirm your membership at the **Member** grade, effective April 1, 2024.

The Member grade is available to anyone with a professional interest in robotic manipulation who pays the annual dues. Elevation to Senior Member or Fellow requires a separate nomination and is open only after five years of membership.

Your membership includes access to the Society's journal, member pricing for the annual symposium, and our technical committees.
