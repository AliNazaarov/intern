# Pulsewright Diagnostics, Inc. - Founder Services Agreement (excerpt)
Effective date: **February 1, 2021**

Pulsewright Diagnostics, Inc., a Delaware corporation incorporated on January 19, 2021 (the "Company"), and Yusuf Brennholt, Ph.D. ("Founder") agree as follows:

**1. Role.** Founder shall serve as the Company's **Chief Executive Officer** and as a member of the Board of Directors.

**2. Intellectual property.** Founder assigns to the Company all rights in inventions relating to wearable ultrasound monitoring conceived during the term of this Agreement.

**3. Compensation.** Founder's salary shall be set by the Board and shall initially be $0 until the Company's first priced financing.

Signed: Yusuf Brennholt; for the Company, Hilde Varga, Director.
