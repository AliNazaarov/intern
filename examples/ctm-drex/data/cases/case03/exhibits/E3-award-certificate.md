# Kendall Biotech Society

## Early Career Investigator Award - 2023

The Kendall Biotech Society is pleased to present its 2023 Early Career Investigator Award to

**Dr. Hana Kowalczyk-Ito**

in recognition of her contributions to the computational identification of cryptic binding sites in protein targets.

The award is given annually to one investigator within ten years of the doctoral degree, selected by the Society's Awards Committee from nominations received nationwide. The award carries an honorarium of $10,000.

Presented at the Society's Annual Meeting, Cambridge, Massachusetts, on April 20, 2023.

Dr. Felix Amundsen, President
