# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Rafael Quintero-Sato  
**Petitioner:** Parlo Learning, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in speech technology for education

Dear Officer:

Parlo Learning, Inc., an Austin company that offers AI-based language tutoring, petitions for O-1A classification for its co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, Rafael Quintero-Sato. Mr. Quintero-Sato designed the speech technology that lets Parlo give learners instant feedback on their pronunciation.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

Parlo Learning won the Best Use of AI award at the 2022 EdTech Lone Star Awards.

## Criterion 2: Membership in Associations Requiring Outstanding Achievement

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(2) permits a beneficiary to qualify through membership in associations in the field which require outstanding achievements of their members, as judged by recognized national or international experts in the field. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

Mr. Quintero-Sato is a member of the Learning Engineering Collegium, which admits new members only after nomination and review by a committee of senior members.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

Classroom Tech Today published an article about Parlo Learning and Mr. Quintero-Sato in February 2025. The article states that Parlo serves 1.4 million learners. His work has also been featured on national television.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

In 2024, Mr. Quintero-Sato judged the Austin AI Hack, which drew 60 teams.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

Mr. Quintero-Sato designed Parlo's speech-scoring engine. The engine scores pronunciation in under 50 milliseconds.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

He is the sole author of the 2022 workshop paper "Low-Latency Pronunciation Scoring for Mobile Learners."

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

Mr. Quintero-Sato co-founded Parlo Learning and has served as its Chief Technology Officer since April 2021. He manages an engineering department of 29 engineers. Parlo's product is used in more than 400 public school districts.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Mr. Quintero-Sato meets this criterion.

His 2025 base salary is $215,000. His 2025 compensation includes a $60,000 performance bonus. His compensation substantially exceeds that of CTOs at comparable Austin startups.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Mr. Quintero-Sato has risen to the very top of the field of speech technology for education and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
