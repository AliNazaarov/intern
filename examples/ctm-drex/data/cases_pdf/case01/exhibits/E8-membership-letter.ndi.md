# Association for Computational Evaluation

February 12, 2024

Dear Dr. Marwick,

I am delighted to inform you that the Membership Committee has approved your elevation to the grade of **Senior Member** of the Association for Computational Evaluation. Senior Member is the Association's highest grade below Fellow. Candidates must have at least ten years of professional experience in the field, must demonstrate significant performance over at least five of those years, and must be nominated by two current Fellows. Each application is reviewed by a panel of Fellows. Fewer than \(9\%\) of the Association's members hold this grade.

Your certificate will be mailed separately.

With congratulations, Prof. Halvard Engstrom, Chair, Membership Committee
