# Clinical Study Summary - PW-CS-02 (company document)

**Title:** Detection of cardiac abnormalities with the Pulsewright wearable ultrasound patch compared with standard echocardiography
**Sites:** 3 hospitals in Minnesota and Wisconsin
**Enrollment:** **220 patients**, completed March 2024
**Primary endpoint:** sensitivity and specificity for clinically significant left-ventricular dysfunction

**Results:**
- Sensitivity: **91%** (95% CI 85-95%)
- Specificity: 88% (95% CI 82-92%)
- No serious device-related adverse events

Principal investigator: Dr. Orla Fenwick. Sponsor: Pulsewright Diagnostics, Inc. Summary prepared for investors and advisors, June 2024.
