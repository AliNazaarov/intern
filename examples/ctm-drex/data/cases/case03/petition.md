# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Dr. Hana Kowalczyk-Ito  
**Petitioner:** Verdant Fold Therapeutics, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in computational drug discovery

Dear Officer:

Verdant Fold Therapeutics, Inc., a Boston biotechnology company developing small-molecule drugs against hard-to-target proteins, petitions for O-1A classification for its co-founder and Chief Scientific Officer, Dr. Hana Kowalczyk-Ito. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito developed the pocket-mapping approach on which the company's discovery platform is built.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

In 2023, Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito received the Early Career Investigator Award from the Kendall Biotech Society. She was also a finalist for the Lindqvist Prize in Structural Biology in 2022.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

Helix Ledger magazine published a profile of Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito in May 2025. The profile credits her team with identifying five preclinical drug candidates.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

In 2024, Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito reviewed six manuscripts for the Journal of Structural Pharmacology. She serves on the editorial board of the Journal of Structural Pharmacology.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

Her 2021 paper on allosteric pocket mapping has been cited more than 140 times. Verdant Fold received a $1.2 million seed grant from the Massachusetts Life Sciences Seed Fund, with Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito as principal investigator. Her pocket-mapping method is now used by most top-20 pharmaceutical companies.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito is the first author of a 2021 article in the Annals of Molecular Design.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito has served as Chief Scientific Officer of Verdant Fold Therapeutics since September 2022. She directs a research team of 25 scientists. Two research directors report directly to her.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito meets this criterion.

Her 2025 base salary is $290,000. In addition to her base salary, she received a $30,000 retention bonus in 2025. Her total compensation places her among the highest-paid scientific officers at Boston seed-stage biotech companies.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Dr. Kowalczyk-Ito has risen to the very top of the field of computational drug discovery and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
