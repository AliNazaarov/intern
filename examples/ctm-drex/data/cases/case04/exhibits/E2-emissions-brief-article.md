# A Denver startup's sorbent bet
*The Emissions Brief, March 9, 2025 - by Lorna Vasquez*

Cinderline Carbon's pilot plant on the outskirts of Denver looks like a row of shipping containers with fans. Inside, a solid amine sorbent pulls carbon dioxide from the air. The company says the pilot captures 500 tonnes of CO2 per year.

The sorbent chemistry was developed at Professor Ruth Calder's university laboratory, where CEO Tomasz Abara was a doctoral researcher. Abara contributed to the sorbent work as one of several students in the group before founding Cinderline to commercialize it under a license from the university.

"My job now is less chemistry and more getting steel in the ground," Abara said. He wants a 10,000-tonne plant by 2028.
