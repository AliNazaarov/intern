# Continuous Left-Ventricular Monitoring with a Conformal Ultrasound Patch: A Fe asibility Study

M. Garrow, **Y. Brennholt**, O. Fenwick, A. Pell

*Cardiac Imaging Letters*, 2023; 9(4): 301-309

**Abstract.** We assessed a conformal ultrasound patch for continuous imaging of the left ventricle in 24 healthy volunteers over 8 hours. Ejection fraction estimates from the patch agreed with conventional echocardiography within 4.1 percentage points (mean absolute difference). Image quality was adequate for automated analysis in \(92\%\) of frames. Results support further clinical study in heart failure patients.
