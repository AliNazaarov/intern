# Northeast Fintech Awards 2024

## Winner - Best SMB Finance Product

Presented to **Tallyrun, Inc.**

for its receivables forecasting platform, selected by an independent panel of fourteen judges from banking, payments, and venture capital from 96 entries in the category.

Presented in Boston, Massachusetts, on November 7, 2024.

*This award recognizes a company and its product. Individual honors are presented separately in the Founder and Leadership categories.*

Signed,
Ilse Montgomery, Executive Director, Northeast Fintech Awards
