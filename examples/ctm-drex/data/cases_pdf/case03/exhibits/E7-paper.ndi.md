# Allosteric Pocket Mapping by Enhanced-Sampling Dynamics

**H. Kowalczyk-Ito**, J. Brandvold, S. Achterberg, M. Fujimoto

*Annals of Molecular Design*, vol. 14, pp. 211-229 (2021)

**Abstract.** Cryptic binding pockets appear only transiently in protein conformational ensembles and are missed by static structure analysis. We introduce an enhanced-sampling workflow that ranks transient pockets by druggability and persistence, and we validate it on 38 targets with known cryptic sites, recovering 31. Prospective application to two targets yielded binders with micromolar affinity.
