# Selective Leaching of Lithium from Mixed Cathode Materials Using Organic Acids

**O. Velde**, S. Brannigan, T. Ochieng, L. Ferreira

*Journal of Separation Chemistry*, 2020, 41: 1150-1163

**Abstract.** We report a two-stage leaching process that selectively recovers lithium from mixed lithium-ion cathode materials using citric and oxalic acids. Lithium selectivity exceeded 97% while transition metals remained in the solid residue for separate recovery. Reagent costs were estimated at 40% of those of a sulfuric acid baseline.
