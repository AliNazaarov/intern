# United States Patent Application Publication

**Pub. No.:** US 2023/0188214 A1 (fictional, for benchmark use)
**Pub. Date:** June 15, 2023
**Title:** SYSTEMS AND METHODS FOR PROBABILISTIC RECEIVABLES FORECASTING
**Applicant:** Tallyrun, Inc., New York, NY
**Inventors:** Mateo Ferreira-Lind, New York, NY; Ana Reyes, Brooklyn, NY
**Filed:** December 12, 2022

**Abstract:** A system receives invoice records for a business and, for each open invoice, estimates a probability distribution over the payment date using features of the payer's historical payment behavior. The distributions are aggregated into a forward cash balance forecast with confidence intervals, and alerts are generated when the forecasted balance falls below a threshold set by the user.
