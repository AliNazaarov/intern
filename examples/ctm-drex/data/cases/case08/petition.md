# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Dr. Yusuf Brennholt  
**Petitioner:** Pulsewright Diagnostics, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in medical device engineering

Dear Officer:

Pulsewright Diagnostics, Inc., a Minneapolis company developing a wearable ultrasound patch for cardiac monitoring, petitions for O-1A classification for its founder and Chief Executive Officer, Dr. Yusuf Brennholt. Dr. Brennholt is a biomedical engineer whose device work has drawn attention from clinicians and funders.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

In 2024, Pulsewright won the Upper Midwest Health Innovation Challenge. The award included a $1 million grant.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

MedDevice Observer published an article on Dr. Brennholt and Pulsewright in September 2024.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

In 2025, Dr. Brennholt served on the grant review panel of the Midwest Medical Device Grants program. He evaluated 14 grant applications in that role.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

Pulsewright's wearable ultrasound patch was evaluated in a clinical study of 220 patients. The study found that the patch detected cardiac abnormalities with 99 percent sensitivity. The device has been adopted by twelve hospital systems.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

Dr. Brennholt is the first author of a 2023 article in Cardiac Imaging Letters.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

Dr. Brennholt founded Pulsewright Diagnostics in 2021 and serves as its Chief Executive Officer. Pulsewright has 27 employees. Dr. Brennholt is the sole inventor of the Pulsewright patch. The company is valued at $150 million.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Dr. Brennholt meets this criterion.

Dr. Brennholt's 2025 salary is $198,000. His salary is in the top decile for medical device executives in Minnesota.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Dr. Brennholt has risen to the very top of the field of medical device engineering and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
