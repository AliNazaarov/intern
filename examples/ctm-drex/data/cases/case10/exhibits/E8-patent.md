# U.S. Patent Application Publication (summary)

**Publication No.:** US 2024/0102917 A1 (fictional, for benchmark use)
**Title:** PROCESS FOR SELECTIVE LITHIUM LEACHING FROM MIXED BATTERY FEEDSTOCK
**Applicant:** Ampcycle Materials, Inc., Reno, NV
**Inventor:** Oskar Velde, Reno, NV
**Filed:** May 22, 2023
**Published:** March 28, 2024

**Abstract:** A process in which shredded battery material is treated with an organic acid solution under controlled temperature to dissolve lithium selectively, followed by precipitation of lithium carbonate. The process reduces the amount of transition metals carried into the lithium stream and lowers reagent consumption.
