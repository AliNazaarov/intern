# The app that listens back
*Classroom Tech Today, February 11, 2025 - by Harriet Mwale*

Language apps have long been good at vocabulary drills and bad at speaking practice. Parlo Learning, an Austin startup, is betting that instant pronunciation feedback is the missing piece.

Parlo's co-founder and CTO, Rafael Quintero-Sato, designed the company's speech-scoring engine, which compares a learner's speech with native-speaker models sound by sound. The app now serves 1.4 million learners, most of them adults learning English or Spanish, the company says.

"People stop practicing speaking because nobody tells them what went wrong," Quintero-Sato said. "We tell them in under a second."

Parlo has raised $14 million to date.
