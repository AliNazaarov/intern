# Index Triggers for Residential Flood Insurance: Design and Basis Risk

**Brightharbor Technical Whitepaper No. 1, October 2023**
Authors: Keiko Arnesen-Wade, Tariq Holloway

**Summary.** Parametric flood products pay a fixed amount when an index crosses a threshold, which makes claims fast but introduces basis risk: a policyholder may suffer damage without a payout, or receive a payout without damage. We describe a trigger based on a network of street-level water sensors and estimate basis risk using 14 years of historical flood records for Miami-Dade County. Under our design, the estimated probability of a damaging flood without a payout falls to 6%.
