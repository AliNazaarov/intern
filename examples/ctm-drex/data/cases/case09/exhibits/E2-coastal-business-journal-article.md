# Flood cover that pays in days, not months
*Coastal Business Journal, April 7, 2025 - by Dario Fuentes*

When a flood hits, traditional insurance claims can take months. Brightharbor Insurance Labs, founded in Miami by actuary Keiko Arnesen-Wade, pays out automatically when water-level sensors near a policyholder's home pass a set threshold.

Arnesen-Wade spent nine years pricing catastrophe risk before starting the company. Brightharbor has issued about 18,000 policies, mostly to homeowners and small shops in South Florida, she said.

"People don't need an adjuster to tell them their street flooded," Arnesen-Wade said. "They need money for a hotel and a new water heater."
