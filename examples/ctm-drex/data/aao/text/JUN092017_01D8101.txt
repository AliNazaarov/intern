PETITION: FORM I-129, PETITION FOR A NONIMMIGRANT WORKER 
The Petitioner, a talent agency, seeks to represent the Beneficiary during his temporary employment in 
the United States as a Dancesport instructor. To do so, the Petitioner seeks to extend the Beneficiary's 
0-1 nonimmigrant visa classification, available to foreign nationals who can demonstrate their 
extraordinary ability through sustained national or international acclaim and whose achievements 
have been recognized in the field through extensive documentation. See Immigration and 
Nationality Act section 101(a)(15)(0)(i), 8 U.S.C. § 1101(a)(15)(0)(i). 
The Director of the Vermont Service Center denied the petition, concluding that the Petitioner did 
not satisfy, as required, the evidentiary criteria applicable to ·individuals of extraordinary ability. in 
the arts. 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(3)(iv)(A)-(B). The Director further determined that the Petitioner did 
not provide an itinerary. See 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(o)(2)(ii)(C), (iv)(A), and (iv)(E)(2). The matter is 
now before us on appeal. 
A review of U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services records indicates that, as ofFebruary 13, 2017, 
the Beneficiary has adjusted status to that of a lawful permanent resident. Accordingly, the issues in 
this proceeding are moot and we dismiss the appeal. 
ORDER: The appeal is dismissed.
