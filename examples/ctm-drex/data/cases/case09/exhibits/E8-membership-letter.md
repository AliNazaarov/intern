# Actuarial Guild of the Americas

November 18, 2019

Dear Ms. Arnesen-Wade,

Congratulations! The Board of the Actuarial Guild of the Americas confirms that you have completed all requirements for the designation of **Fellow** of the Guild (FAGA).

Fellowship requires passing **nine professional examinations**, completing a professionalism course, and at least three years of supervised actuarial work. Examination pass rates average below 45%, and fewer than one in five candidates who begin the examination sequence reach Fellowship.

You may now use the FAGA designation.

Sincerely,
Registrar, Actuarial Guild of the Americas
