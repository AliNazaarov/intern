# The startup grading the graders
*Byteforge Weekly, August 14, 2024 - by Corinne Haddad*

When a language model grades another language model, who checks the grader? That question is the founding premise of Quillmetric AI, a two-year-old San Francisco company co-founded by researcher Lena Marwick.

Marwick, who previously worked on evaluation infrastructure at a cloud provider, says the industry relies too heavily on single automated judges. "Judges drift," she told Byteforge. "If you don't measure that drift, your leaderboard is fiction."

Quillmetric's platform runs panels of evaluators and flags disagreement. The company says the platform is now used by about 340 companies, from small startups to large banks. In June, Quillmetric raised an $18 million Series A led by Northfold Capital.

Marwick oversees the company's research and engineering. She says the next step is evaluation for multi-step agents, which she calls "the unsolved problem of the next three years."
