# # Kestrelbay Robotics - 2025 Harvest Season Field Report (internal, excerpt)

**Prepared by:** Abebe Mulugeta, Head of Field Operations
**Date:** October 20, 2025

- Machines deployed: 8, across 3 partner farms in the Yakima Valley

- Apples harvested by robot: approximately 1.9 million

- Bruise rate: 2.4% (hand-picking benchmark at partner farms: 3.1%)

- Average picks per arm per hour: 410

**Notes:** The new branch-avoidance motion planner, developed by the Motion pod under Mira Okafor-Lund's technical leadership, cut arm collisions with branches by 63% compared with the 2024 season. Field technicians reported fewer manual re sets as a result.
