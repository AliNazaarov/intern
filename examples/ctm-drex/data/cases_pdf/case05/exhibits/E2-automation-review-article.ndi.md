# Teaching warehouse robots to feel
*Automation Review Quarterly, October 2024 - by Benedikt Rao*

Picking a single item from a jumbled bin is still one of the hardest problems in warehouse automation. Pittsburgh-based Lumacrate Robotics says it has made real progress, and much of the credit goes to Dr. Ifeoma Strand, who led the develop ment of the company's tactile-visual perception system.
Strand's approach fuses camera data with readings from soft fingertip sensors, so the gripper can adjust when an item slips. Lumacrate says its robots now pick about 1,200 items per hour in customer deployments, with a 99.2 percent success rate.

"Cameras tell you where to reach," Strand said. "Touch tells you whether you act ually have it."
