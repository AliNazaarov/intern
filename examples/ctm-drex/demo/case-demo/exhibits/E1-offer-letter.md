# Kestrelbay Robotics, Inc. - Offer Letter

March 4, 2024

Dear Mira,

We are delighted to offer you the position of **Founding Engineer** at Kestrelbay Robotics, Inc., starting **March 18, 2024**. You will report to **Theo Vance, Chief Technology Officer**.

Your annual base salary will be **$182,000**, paid semi-monthly. You will be granted an option to purchase 0.8% of the Company's fully diluted shares, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff.

As Founding Engineer you will be responsible for the motion-planning software of our autonomous orchard-harvesting robot and will serve as technical lead of the Motion pod. People management (hiring decisions, performance reviews) remains with the CTO.

Sincerely,
Ines Calloway, Co-Founder and CEO
