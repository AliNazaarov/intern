# Designing calm: inside Fieldglass Studio
*Formline Magazine, June 2024 - Studio Visits, by Aurelio Banks*

Fieldglass Studio occupies the top floor of a converted print shop in Los Angeles. Founder Noor Halvorsen keeps it deliberately small: eleven designers, no account managers, and a rule that every designer talks to real patients before drawing a screen.

The approach has paid off. Fieldglass has worked with about 30 health and wellness clients since 2019, and its Kinwell app for family caregivers has become a reference point for accessible design in health care.

"Most health apps are designed for the healthiest person in the room," Halvorsen said. "We design for the person who is tired and scared at two in the morning."
