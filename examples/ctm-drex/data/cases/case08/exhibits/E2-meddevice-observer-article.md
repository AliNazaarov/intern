# A stethoscope you stick on
*MedDevice Observer, September 18, 2024 - by Collins Avery*

Pulsewright Diagnostics wants cardiac ultrasound to leave the hospital. The Minneapolis company's flexible patch, about the size of a credit card, captures images of the heart continuously for up to 72 hours.

Founder and CEO Dr. Yusuf Brennholt is one of three co-inventors of the patch, along with Pulsewright CTO Dr. Mei-Lin Garrow and Dr. Arnav Pell of the university lab where the idea began. Brennholt led the work on the patch's flexible transducer array.

The company recently completed a clinical study and says it plans to seek regulatory clearance next year. "Heart failure patients get worse at home, not in the clinic," Brennholt said. "That is where we need to be watching."
