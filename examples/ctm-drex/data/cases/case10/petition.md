# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Dr. Oskar Velde  
**Petitioner:** Ampcycle Materials, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in battery materials recycling

Dear Officer:

Ampcycle Materials, Inc., a Reno company that recovers lithium and other metals from spent batteries, petitions for O-1A classification for Dr. Oskar Velde, its Vice President of Engineering. Dr. Velde is a materials scientist whose research on selective leaching informs Ampcycle's recovery process.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

In 2024, Dr. Velde received the Sierra Materials Innovation Award.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

Gridbeat published an article in November 2024 describing Dr. Velde's work at Ampcycle. The article reports that Ampcycle's plant can process 30,000 tonnes of batteries per year. According to the article, Ampcycle's process recovers 95 percent of the lithium in spent batteries.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

In 2025, Dr. Velde reviewed four manuscripts for the Journal of Circular Materials. He is a member of the editorial board of the Journal of Circular Materials.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

Dr. Velde independently designed Ampcycle's entire hydrometallurgy line. He is the named inventor on a 2023 patent application for a selective lithium leaching process. His process has reduced industry-wide recycling costs by 40 percent.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

Dr. Velde is the first author of a 2020 article on selective leaching that has been cited 210 times.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

Dr. Velde co-founded Ampcycle Materials and serves as its Chief Executive Officer. He leads a team of 23 engineers. He joined Ampcycle in July 2022.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Dr. Velde meets this criterion.

His 2025 base salary is $250,000. He was offered a $400,000 position by a competing firm, which he declined.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Dr. Velde has risen to the very top of the field of battery materials recycling and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
