# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Tomasz Abara  
**Petitioner:** Cinderline Carbon, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in carbon removal engineering

Dear Officer:

Cinderline Carbon, Inc., a Denver company that builds direct air capture systems using solid sorbents, petitions for O-1A classification for its founder and Chief Executive Officer, Tomasz Abara. Mr. Abara has taken the company from a university spin-out to an operating pilot plant and has become a visible figure in the carbon removal sector.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

Mr. Abara's company won first place in the 2024 Rocky Mountain Cleantech Prize. The prize carried a cash award of $50,000.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

The Emissions Brief featured Mr. Abara and Cinderline Carbon in a March 2025 article. The article reports that Cinderline's pilot plant captures 500 tonnes of carbon dioxide per year.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

In 2025, Mr. Abara served on the judging panel of the Climate Venture Cup, evaluating 18 startups.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

Mr. Abara single-handedly invented Cinderline's sorbent chemistry. The sorbent reduces energy use by 60 percent compared with leading competitors.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

Mr. Abara co-authored a 2022 conference paper on amine-functionalized sorbents. He is the lead author of that paper.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

Mr. Abara founded Cinderline Carbon in 2020 and serves as its Chief Executive Officer. Under his leadership, Cinderline closed a $9.5 million seed round in 2024. The company has 22 employees. Cinderline holds offtake agreements with two Fortune 500 companies.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Mr. Abara meets this criterion.

Mr. Abara's 2025 salary is $250,000. His equity stake was independently valued at $4 million in 2025.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Mr. Abara has risen to the very top of the field of carbon removal engineering and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
