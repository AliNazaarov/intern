# Squeezing lithium out of yesterday's batteries
*Gridbeat, November 5, 2024 - by Ottilie Grace*

Ampcycle Materials' facility east of Reno takes in spent electric-vehicle and electronics batteries and sends out battery-grade lithium carbonate. The plant can process about 3,000 tonnes of batteries per year, and the company says its process recovers 95 percent of the lithium.
Dr. Oskar Velde, Ampcycle's vice president of engineering, helped design the pla nt's hydrometallurgy line together with a team of process engineers and an outsi de engineering firm. His earlier research on selective leaching shaped the chemi stry.

"Recycling only matters if the output is good enough to go back into a battery," Velde said.
