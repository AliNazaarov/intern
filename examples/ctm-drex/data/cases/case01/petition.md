# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Dr. Lena Marwick  
**Petitioner:** Quillmetric AI, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in artificial intelligence evaluation

Dear Officer:

Quillmetric AI, Inc., a San Francisco software company that builds evaluation tooling for large language models, petitions for O-1A classification for Dr. Lena Marwick, its co-founder and technical leader. Dr. Marwick is a recognized authority on measuring the reliability of automated model judges, and her work is used by engineering teams across the industry.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

In 2024, Dr. Marwick was named the winner of the Applied AI Innovator of the Year award at the Pacific AI Builders Forum. She also received the Golden Gate Founders Medal in 2022 for her early work on model auditing.

## Criterion 2: Membership in Associations Requiring Outstanding Achievement

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(2) permits a beneficiary to qualify through membership in associations in the field which require outstanding achievements of their members, as judged by recognized national or international experts in the field. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

In February 2024, Dr. Marwick was elevated to Senior Member of the Association for Computational Evaluation, a grade that requires nomination by two Fellows. Senior Member status requires at least ten years of professional experience in the field.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

Byteforge Weekly published a feature article about Dr. Marwick and Quillmetric in August 2024. The article reports that Quillmetric's evaluation platform is used by more than 3,400 companies. The same article notes that Quillmetric raised an $18 million Series A round led by Northfold Capital.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

In 2025, Dr. Marwick was invited to serve as a final-round judge at the Bay Neural Hackathon, where she evaluated 42 competing teams. She has also served as a peer reviewer for the Journal of Machine Reasoning since 2021.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

Dr. Marwick's paper on evaluator drift in LLM judges has been cited 87 times.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

She is the sole author of the paper "Calibrated Disagreement: Measuring Evaluator Drift in LLM Judges," published in 2023.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

Dr. Marwick has served as Chief Technology Officer of Quillmetric AI since March 2023. As CTO, she leads an engineering organization of 40 people. She reports directly to the company's Chief Executive Officer, Daniel Osei.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Dr. Marwick meets this criterion.

Her 2025 annualized base salary is $240,000. This salary places her in the top 5 percent of software executives in the San Francisco area.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Dr. Marwick has risen to the very top of the field of artificial intelligence evaluation and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
