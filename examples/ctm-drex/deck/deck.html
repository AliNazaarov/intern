The problem

An O-1A packet runs to about 800 pages, and one unbacked sentence costs months

Request for Evidence

One claim its exhibit doesn't back, or contradicts, and the officer sends a Request for Evidence. The case waits months.

Confident drafts

AI drafting agents write fluent, confident sentences that their own exhibits don't support.

Checked once, by hand

Frontier models are too slow and too costly to check every sentence, so checking happens once, at the end, by a person.

ⓘ Motivating firm: Expedite, an AI-native startup law firm. It is not a user of this build; every case here is synthetic.
