# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Mateo Ferreira-Lind  
**Petitioner:** Tallyrun, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in financial technology

Dear Officer:

Tallyrun, Inc., a New York financial technology company that provides cash-flow forecasting to small businesses, petitions for O-1A classification for its founder, Mateo Ferreira-Lind. Mr. Ferreira-Lind designed the forecasting engine at the center of the company's product and has led Tallyrun from a two-person project to an established provider.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

In 2024, Tallyrun won the Best SMB Finance Product award at the Northeast Fintech Awards. Mr. Ferreira-Lind was personally named to the Forty Under Forty in Finance list by Meridian Business Review in 2023.

## Criterion 2: Membership in Associations Requiring Outstanding Achievement

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(2) permits a beneficiary to qualify through membership in associations in the field which require outstanding achievements of their members, as judged by recognized national or international experts in the field. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

Mr. Ferreira-Lind is a member of the Guild of Financial Technologists, which admits only professionals with outstanding achievements as judged by recognized experts.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

In January 2025, The Ledgerline published an article profiling Mr. Ferreira-Lind and Tallyrun's forecasting engine. According to The Ledgerline, Tallyrun serves approximately 9,000 small businesses. The article reports that Tallyrun processes $21 billion in monthly invoice volume.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

In 2024, Mr. Ferreira-Lind served as a judge for the Hudson Valley Startup Challenge.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

Mr. Ferreira-Lind personally built the first version of Tallyrun's forecasting model. He is a named inventor on a published U.S. patent application for systems for probabilistic receivables forecasting. Tallyrun's forecasting method has been adopted as an industry standard by three regional banks.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

Mr. Ferreira-Lind founded Tallyrun in June 2021 and has served as its Chief Executive Officer since founding. Tallyrun currently employs 31 full-time staff. The company's Chief Technology Officer, Ana Reyes, reports to the Board rather than to Mr. Ferreira-Lind.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Mr. Ferreira-Lind meets this criterion.

His 2024 W-2 wages from Tallyrun totaled $195,000. This compensation exceeds the 90th percentile for chief executives of companies of comparable size in New York.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Mr. Ferreira-Lind has risen to the very top of the field of financial technology and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
