# Pacific Design Honors 2024
## Gold Award - Digital Product
Awarded to **Fieldglass Studio** (Design Director: Noor Halvorsen) for **Kinwell**, a caregiving coordination app.
Selected by the professional jury from 1,380 entries across nine categories. Gold Awards are given to the top entries in each category.
**Grand Prize 2024:** awarded to Marrow & Pike Studio for "Tidepool."
Presented in Los Angeles on November 15, 2024.
Signed,
Jasmine Ocampo, Jury Chair
