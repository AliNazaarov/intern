# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Dr. Ifeoma Strand  
**Petitioner:** Lumacrate Robotics, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in robotic perception

Dear Officer:

Lumacrate Robotics, Inc., a Pittsburgh company that builds robotic picking systems for warehouses, petitions for O-1A classification for Dr. Ifeoma Strand. Dr. Strand is an expert in combining touch and vision so that robots can pick items from cluttered bins, and her work underpins Lumacrate's core product.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Dr. Strand's paper received the Best Paper Award at the 2023 International Workshop on Grasping.

## Criterion 2: Membership in Associations Requiring Outstanding Achievement

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(2) permits a beneficiary to qualify through membership in associations in the field which require outstanding achievements of their members, as judged by recognized national or international experts in the field. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Dr. Strand is a member of the Society for Robotic Manipulation. She was elected a Fellow of the Society for Robotic Manipulation in 2024.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Automation Review Quarterly published an article in October 2024 describing Dr. Strand's work on Lumacrate's vision system. The article reports that Lumacrate's robots pick 12,000 items per hour.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Dr. Strand served on the program committee of the 2025 Conference on Embodied Perception, where she reviewed nine papers.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Dr. Strand led the development of Lumacrate's tactile-visual perception system. The system has been licensed by two other robotics companies.

## Criterion 6: Authorship of Scholarly Articles

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(6) permits a beneficiary to qualify through authorship of scholarly articles in the field, in professional journals or other major media. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

She is the first author of the 2023 paper "Tactile-Visual Fusion for Cluttered Bin Picking."

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Dr. Strand serves as Chief Technology Officer of Lumacrate Robotics. She joined Lumacrate in January 2022. She reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer. Her perception team comprises nine engineers.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Dr. Strand meets this criterion.

Her 2025 base salary is $228,000. She also holds stock options valued at more than $1 million. Her salary is more than double the median wage for robotics engineers in Pittsburgh.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Dr. Strand has risen to the very top of the field of robotic perception and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
