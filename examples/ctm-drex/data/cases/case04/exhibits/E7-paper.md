# Cycling Stability of Amine-Functionalized Silica Sorbents Under Humid Air

Y. Sall, P. Ortega, **T. Abara**, K. Mensah, R. Calder

*Proceedings of the 2022 Conference on Negative Emissions Engineering*, pp. 88-95

**Abstract.** We report the degradation behavior of amine-functionalized silica sorbents over 1,200 adsorption-desorption cycles under humid air at 25 C. Capacity loss was limited to 11% when regeneration temperature was kept below 95 C. We discuss implications for low-temperature regeneration in modular direct air capture units.

*Author contributions: Y.S. and P.O. designed and performed the experiments; T.A. contributed to cycling test setup and data analysis; R.C. supervised the project.*
