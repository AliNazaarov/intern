The problem · O-1A & EB-1A visas

One unsupported sentence
can sink a visa.

Founders and researchers apply for the O-1A “extraordinary ability” visa with a petition that makes hundreds of claims about them. Every claim must point to proof. When a sentence says more than its evidence shows, the officer asks for more evidence, or says no.

What a petition is

A letter of a few dozen pages“She led the motion team.” “He won the award.”
Hundreds of claimstitles, salaries, team sizes, press, awards
Exhibits behind each oneoffer letters, org charts, payroll; packets run to hundreds of pages

Since 5 August 2026 (USCIS Policy Alert PA-2026-05), an officer may deny a petition that is missing required initial evidence without first asking for it.
