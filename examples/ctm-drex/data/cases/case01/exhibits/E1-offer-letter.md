# Quillmetric AI, Inc. - Offer of Employment

February 20, 2023

Dear Lena,

On behalf of the Board of Directors, I am pleased to offer you the position of **Chief Technology Officer** of Quillmetric AI, Inc. (the "Company"), starting on **March 6, 2023**. In this role you will report directly to me, Daniel Osei, Chief Executive Officer.

Your starting base salary will be $210,000 per year, paid semi-monthly, subject to standard withholdings. Subject to Board approval, you will be granted an option to purchase shares equal to 4.0% of the Company's fully diluted capitalization, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff.

You will be responsible for the Company's technical strategy, research agenda, and engineering hiring. This offer is contingent on verification of your right to work in the United States.

Sincerely,
Daniel Osei, CEO
Accepted: Lena Marwick, February 22, 2023
