# Operating Agreement of Fieldglass Studio LLC (excerpt)

This Operating Agreement is made effective as of **May 2, 2019** by Noor Halvorsen as the **sole Member** of Fieldglass Studio LLC, a California limited liability company (the "Company").

**Section 2.1 Formation.** The Company was formed by filing Articles of Organization with the California Secretary of State on April 26, 2019.

**Section 5.1 Management.** The Company shall be managed by its Member. Noor Halvorsen is designated as the **sole Managing Member** and shall have full authority to bind the Company.

**Section 6.2 Guaranteed Payments.** The Managing Member may receive guaranteed payments for services, in amounts determined annually and recorded in the Company's books.

Executed by Noor Halvorsen, Managing Member.
