# Learning Engineering Collegium

June 9, 2023

Dear Mr. Quintero-Sato,

It is my pleasure to inform you that you have been admitted as a Member of the Learning Engineering Collegium.

Membership in the Collegium is by invitation only. Each candidate must be nominated by an existing member, and nominations are reviewed by the Admissions Committee, which consists of seven senior members recognized for their contributions to learning science and engineering. This year the Committee admitted 22 of 140 nominees.

We look forward to your participation.

Sincerely,
Dr. Marguerite Ishikawa, Chair, Admissions Committee
