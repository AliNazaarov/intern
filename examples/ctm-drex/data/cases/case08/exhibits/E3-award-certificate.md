# Upper Midwest Health Innovation Challenge 2024

## Winner

**Pulsewright Diagnostics, Inc.**
Team lead: Dr. Yusuf Brennholt

The Upper Midwest Health Innovation Challenge is an annual competition for early-stage health technology companies. The 2024 Challenge received 133 applications. The winner receives a non-dilutive grant of **$100,000** and a year of mentoring from regional health systems.

Awarded in Minneapolis, Minnesota, on May 22, 2024.

Signed,
Dr. Sunniva Bakke, Chair, Selection Committee
