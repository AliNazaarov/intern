# Tallyrun, Inc. - Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors

Effective June 14, 2021

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors of Tallyrun, Inc., a Delaware corporation incorporated on June 9, 2021, hereby adopt the following resolutions:

**RESOLVED**, that Mateo Ferreira-Lind, founder of the Corporation, is hereby appointed **Chief Executive Officer** and President of the Corporation, to serve at the pleasure of the Board;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer is authorized to hire employees, open bank accounts, and enter into commercial agreements on behalf of the Corporation in the ordinary course of business.

Directors: Mateo Ferreira-Lind; Gwen Achterberg; Samuel Oduya
