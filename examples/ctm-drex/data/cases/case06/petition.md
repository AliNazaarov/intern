# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Noor Halvorsen  
**Petitioner:** Fieldglass Studio LLC  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in digital product design

Dear Officer:

Fieldglass Studio LLC, a Los Angeles design studio that creates digital products for health and wellbeing companies, petitions for O-1A classification for its founder and Design Director, Noor Halvorsen. Ms. Halvorsen's interface work has been recognized by her peers and used by millions of people.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Ms. Halvorsen meets this criterion.

In 2024, Ms. Halvorsen's Kinwell app design received the Gold Award in the Digital Product category of the Pacific Design Honors. The Kinwell design also received the Pacific Design Honors Grand Prize.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Ms. Halvorsen meets this criterion.

Formline Magazine published a profile of Ms. Halvorsen in June 2024. The profile describes Fieldglass Studio as a team of 45 designers.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Ms. Halvorsen meets this criterion.

Ms. Halvorsen was invited to judge the student category of the 2025 Pacific Design Honors. She chaired the professional jury of the 2025 Pacific Design Honors.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Ms. Halvorsen meets this criterion.

The Kinwell app, designed by Fieldglass under Ms. Halvorsen's direction, has been downloaded more than two million times. Her accessible color system has been adopted by the State of California's digital services team. She delivered a keynote address at the Interface Futures conference in 2023.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Ms. Halvorsen meets this criterion.

Ms. Halvorsen founded Fieldglass Studio in 2019 and is its sole managing member. Fieldglass Studio employs 11 designers. Fieldglass's revenue grew 300 percent between 2022 and 2024.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Ms. Halvorsen meets this criterion.

Ms. Halvorsen received guaranteed payments of $165,000 from Fieldglass Studio in 2024. Her guaranteed payments for 2025 are set at $210,000. This places her compensation above the 95th percentile for design directors in Los Angeles.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Ms. Halvorsen has risen to the very top of the field of digital product design and has sustained national acclaim. We respectfully request that the petition be approved. [Paralegal note: attorney to review exhibit citations before filing.]
