# O-1A petition draft (Intern, round 1, before the rewrite)
## Mira Okafor-Lund · Kestrelbay Robotics, Inc. (fictional)

The Beneficiary, Ms. Mira Okafor-Lund, is employed by the Petitioner, Kestrelbay Robotics, Inc. ("Kestrelbay" or the "Company"), in a critical and essential capacity.

As set forth below, the Beneficiary holds the position of Founding Engineer, serves as the technical lead of the Company's Motion pod, and has been directly responsible for the development of the motion-planning technology that enables the Company's autonomous orchard-harvesting robots to perform their commercial work.

The Beneficiary's Role and Responsibilities**

By offer letter dated March 4, 2024, the Company offered the Beneficiary the position of Founding Engineer, effective March 18, 2024, with the Beneficiary's acceptance on March 5, 2024 (E1-offer-letter.md).

As Founding Engineer, the Beneficiary is responsible for the motion-planning software of the Company's autonomous orchard-harvesting robot and serves as technical lead of the Motion pod, reporting to Theo Vance, Chief Technology Officer (E1-offer-letter.md).

The September 2025 organizational chart confirms that the Beneficiary is the designated technical lead of the Motion pod, a team of six engineers (E3-org-chart.md).

The Beneficiary is one of the Company's earliest hires (E2-press-article.md).

Press coverage in the trade publication *Orchard & Machine Weekly* describes the Beneficiary as "one of the minds behind the robot's motion planning, which lets the arm reach around branches instead of through them," and reports that colleagues say she has helped build a team of dozens since joining in 2024 (E2-press-article.md).

Essential Contribution to the Company's Products and Operations**

The Company's 2025 Harvest Season Field Report, prepared by Abebe Mulugeta, Head of Field Operations, establishes that the Beneficiary's technical leadership is directly tied to the Company's operational success.

The report states that the branch-avoidance motion planner — developed by the Motion pod under the Beneficiary's technical leadership — reduced arm collisions with branches by 63% compared with the 2024 season, and that field technicians reported fewer manual resets as a result (E6-field-report.md).

During the 2025 season, eight machines deployed across three partner farms in the Yakima Valley harvested approximately 1.9 million apples at a bruise rate of 2.4% (against a hand-picking benchmark of 3.1%) and an average of 410 picks per arm per hour (E6-field-report.md).

The Company's CEO, Ines Calloway, expressly affirmed the Beneficiary's essential role in writing: "You've been essential to getting the harvesters through this season" (E5-email-comp-discussion.md).

Distinguished Reputation of the Petitioner**

The Petitioner is a recognized and growing participant in its field.

In June 2025, the trade publication *Orchard & Machine Weekly* profiled the Company's harvesting technology and its deployment of eight machines on three farms in the Yakima Valley (E2-press-article.md).

The same article reports that Kestrelbay raised a $12 million Series A in 2024 (E2-press-article.md).

These distinctions reflect the Petitioner's distinguished reputation in autonomous agricultural robotics, and the Beneficiary holds a leading engineering position within that organization.

The Beneficiary receives high remuneration for her services to the Petitioner.

The Beneficiary's annual base salary is $182,000, paid semi-monthly (E1-offer-letter.md).

The Company's Employee Earnings Statement for the period January 1, 2025 through October 31, 2025, confirms that the annual base salary rate of $182,000 has remained unchanged since hire, and that the Beneficiary received in base salary alone $151,666.70 in the ten months of 2025 reported, for a gross pay YTD of $151,666.70 (E4-payroll-2025.md).

In addition to base salary, the Beneficiary was granted an option to purchase 0.8% of the Company's fully diluted shares, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff (E1-offer-letter.md).

The earnings statement notes that equity awards are not included in the salary figures reported (E4-payroll-2025.md).

Company Recognition of the Beneficiary's Compensation Value**

In an email of August 21, 2025, the CEO — in response to a compensation check-in — stated that the Company wants the Beneficiary's pay to "reflect" her essential role, and communicated the Company's goal of bringing the Beneficiary to a $250,000 package in 2026, a goal the CEO candidly described as contingent on closing the Series B and on Board approval of the 2026 compensation plan (E5-email-comp-discussion.md).

This statement, from the Company's highest executive, evidences the Company's own valuation of the Beneficiary's contributions and the upward trajectory of her compensation.
