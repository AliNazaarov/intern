The problem

~1 in 5

of O-1 petitions get a Request for Evidence: months of waiting

33%

Why now: since 5 Aug 2026 (USCIS Policy Alert PA-2026-05), officers may deny a petition missing required evidence without asking first. Fewer second chances.

ⓘ FY2024 O-1 and FY2025 EB-1A counts and RFE rates: secondary analyses of USCIS data (beyondborderglobal.com, f1jobs.io, manifestlaw.com); counts vary by method. 63%: our labels of public USCIS AAO decisions, 381 of 607 failures.
