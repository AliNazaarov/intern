# Tactile-Visual Fusion for Cluttered Bin Picking

**Ifeoma Strand**, Moses Oyelaran

*Proceedings of the International Workshop on Grasping*, 2023

**Abstract.** We present a grasping system that fuses depth images with signals from compliant fingertip sensors to detect and correct slip during the lift phase. On a benchmark of 600 household and retail items in cluttered bins, our method raises grasp success from 91.4% to 98.7% and halves the number of retries compared with a vision-only baseline. We release our tactile dataset.
