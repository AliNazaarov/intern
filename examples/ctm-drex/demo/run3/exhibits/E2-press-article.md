# The robots picking Washington's apples
*Orchard & Machine Weekly, June 2, 2025 - by Felix Durand*

Kestrelbay Robotics' harvesters roll down orchard rows on rubber tracks, using soft grippers to pull apples without bruising them. This season the Yakima Valley startup is running eight machines on three farms.

Engineer Mira Okafor-Lund, one of the company's earliest hires, is one of the minds behind the robot's motion planning, which lets the arm reach around branches instead of through them. Colleagues say she helped build a team of dozens since joining in 2024, though the company declined to share headcount figures.

"Every tree is different," Okafor-Lund said. "The robot has to plan a new path for every apple."

Kestrelbay raised a $12 million Series A in 2024.
