# DRAFT: O-1A Petition Letter (paralegal working draft, not for filing)

**Beneficiary:** Keiko Arnesen-Wade  
**Petitioner:** Brightharbor Insurance Labs, Inc.  
**Classification sought:** O-1A, extraordinary ability in insurance technology and actuarial science

Dear Officer:

Brightharbor Insurance Labs, Inc., a Miami company offering parametric flood insurance, petitions for O-1A classification for its founder and Chief Executive Officer, Keiko Arnesen-Wade. Ms. Arnesen-Wade is a credentialed actuary who designed Brightharbor's index-based flood product.

The beneficiary qualifies under at least three of the evidentiary criteria listed at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Each criterion is addressed in turn below, followed by a discussion of the totality of the record.

## Criterion 1: Nationally or Internationally Recognized Prizes or Awards

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(1) permits a beneficiary to qualify through documentation of the beneficiary's receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence in the field of endeavor. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

Ms. Arnesen-Wade was named Founder of the Year at the 2024 Florida Insurtech Summit.

## Criterion 2: Membership in Associations Requiring Outstanding Achievement

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(2) permits a beneficiary to qualify through membership in associations in the field which require outstanding achievements of their members, as judged by recognized national or international experts in the field. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

Ms. Arnesen-Wade is a Fellow of the Actuarial Guild of the Americas. Fellowship requires passing nine professional examinations.

## Criterion 3: Published Material About the Beneficiary

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(3) permits a beneficiary to qualify through published material in professional or major trade publications or major media about the beneficiary, relating to the beneficiary's work in the field. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

Coastal Business Journal profiled Ms. Arnesen-Wade in April 2025. The article reports that Brightharbor has issued 80,000 policies.

## Criterion 4: Judging the Work of Others

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(4) permits a beneficiary to qualify through participation, either individually or on a panel, as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

In 2025, she served as a judge at Gulf Coast Pitch Night. She has judged the Miami Fintech Week startup competition every year since 2021.

## Criterion 5: Original Contributions of Major Significance

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(5) permits a beneficiary to qualify through original scientific, scholarly, or business-related contributions of major significance in the field. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

Ms. Arnesen-Wade co-authored Brightharbor's 2023 technical whitepaper on index triggers for flood insurance. Her trigger design has been adopted by the state's residual insurance market.

## Criterion 7: Critical or Essential Capacity

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(7) permits a beneficiary to qualify through employment in a critical or essential capacity for organizations and establishments that have a distinguished reputation. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

Ms. Arnesen-Wade founded Brightharbor in 2022 and serves as its Chief Executive Officer. She holds a 75 percent ownership stake in the company. Brightharbor has 16 employees.

## Criterion 8: High Salary or Other Remuneration

The regulation at 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B)(8) permits a beneficiary to qualify through a high salary or other significantly high remuneration for services, as evidenced by contracts or other reliable evidence. Ms. Arnesen-Wade meets this criterion.

Her 2025 compensation includes an annual bonus target of 10 percent of base salary. Her salary was increased to $250,000 effective January 2025. Her total compensation exceeds that of 90 percent of insurance executives in Miami-Dade County.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the evidence shows that Ms. Arnesen-Wade has risen to the very top of the field of insurance technology and actuarial science and has sustained national acclaim.
