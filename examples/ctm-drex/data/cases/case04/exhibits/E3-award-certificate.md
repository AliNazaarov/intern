# Rocky Mountain Cleantech Prize 2024

## Second Place

awarded to **Cinderline Carbon, Inc.** (Tomasz Abara, CEO)

for "Modular Solid-Sorbent Direct Air Capture."

The Rocky Mountain Cleantech Prize is an annual competition for early-stage climate technology companies in the Mountain West. In 2024, 64 companies applied and 8 finalists pitched to the judging panel on September 19, 2024 in Boulder, Colorado.

First Place ($150,000) was awarded to Brightwater Thermal. Second Place carries a cash award of **$50,000**.

Signed on behalf of the Prize Committee,
Avery Lund, Director
