# EdTech Lone Star Awards 2023

## Winner - Best Use of AI

Presented to **Parlo Learning, Inc.**

Accepted on behalf of the team by Rafael Quintero-Sato, Co-Founder and CTO.

The EdTech Lone Star Awards recognize education technology companies based in Texas. Category winners are selected by a panel of educators, researchers, and investors. In 2023 the Best Use of AI category received 58 entries.

Presented in Austin, Texas, on September 28, 2023.

Signed, Board of the EdTech Lone Star Awards
