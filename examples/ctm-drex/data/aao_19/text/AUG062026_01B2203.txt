Appeal of U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) Decision 
Form I-140, Immigrant Petition for Alien Workers (Extraordinary Ability) 
The Petitioner, a fashion stylist, seeks classification as an individual of extraordinary ability. See 
Immigration and Nationality Act (the Act) section 203(b )(1 )(A), 8 U.S .C. § l l 53(b )(1 )(A). This first 
preference classification makes immigrant visas available to those aliens who can demonstrate their 
extraordinary ability through sustained national or international acclaim and whose achievements have 
been recognized in their field through extensive documentation. Id. 
USCIS denied the petition, concluding that the record did not establish that the Petitioner met the 
initial evidentiary requirements through evidence of a one-time achievement or meeting at least three 
of the evidentiary criteria at 8 C.F.R. § 204.5(h)(3). The Petitioner subsequently filed a combined 
motion to reopen and motion to reconsider, which USCIS dismissed as untimely filed. The matter is 
now before us on appeal pursuant to 8 C.F.R. § 103.3. 
The Petitioner bears the burden ofproof to demonstrate eligibility by a preponderance of the evidence. 
Matter ofChawathe, 25 I&N Dec. 369, 375-76 (AAO 2010). We review the questions in this matter 
de novo. Matter of Christa's, Inc., 26 I&N Dec. 537,537 n.2 (AAO 2015). Upon de novo review, 
we will withdraw the decision and remand the matter for entry of a new decision consistent with the 
following analysis. 
The sole issue before us on appeal is whether USCIS properly dismissed the Petitioner's motion to 
reopen and motion to reconsider as untimely filed. Motions to reopen or reconsider must be filed 
within 30 days of the decision, or 33 days if the decision is served by mail. 8 C.F.R. § 103.5(a)(l)(i), 
103.8(b). Here, the record reflects that USCIS' decision was issued on November 28, 2025, and that 
the Petitioner's Form I-290B, Notice of Appeal or Motion, was received by USCIS on December 30, 
2025, 32 days after that decision. 1 Since the Petitioner's Form I-290B was received within 33 days of 
the decision, the motions appear to have been timely filed. Therefore, we will remand the matter for 
USCIS to adjudicate the motions on their merits. 
ORDER: The decision is withdrawn. The matter is remanded for the entry of a new decision 
consistent with the foregoing analysis.
