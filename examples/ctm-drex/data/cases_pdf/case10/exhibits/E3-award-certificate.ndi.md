# Sierra Materials Innovation Award 2024
The Sierra Materials Council is pleased to present the 2024 Sierra Materials Innovation Award to
**Dr. Oskar Velde**
Ampcycle Materials, Inc.
for contributions to selective lithium recovery from mixed battery feedstock.
The award is given annually to one individual working in the Sierra Nevada region whose work has advanced materials science or materials processing. The 2024 recipient was chosen by a committee of nine from 37 nominations.
Presented in Reno, Nevada, on October 17, 2024.
Dr. Annelise Ruppert, President, Sierra Materials Council
